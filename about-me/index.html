About Me

Professional Training & Experience

I trained at Brighton University, earning a BSc in Physiotherapy, and have built a broad foundation of clinical experience working within the NHS across a variety of settings including:

  • Elderly rehabilitation and falls prevention
  • Stroke rehabilitation
  • Cardiothoracic and respiratory care
  • Community rehabilitation
  • Managing long-term conditions and complex care needs

This diverse experience has shaped my patient-centred approach, focusing on functional recovery, empowerment, and whole-person care.

Holistic Therapy & Massage

Alongside physiotherapy, I’m trained in holistic massage and facials, previously offering treatments in both spa environments and clients homes.

My qualifications include:

  • ITEC Diploma in Sports Massage
  • Level 3 CMA/IPHM Holistic Body Massage
  • Level 2 CMA/IPHM Holistic Facials

My passion for holistic therapy stems from a deep belief in the importance of gratitude, self-care, and restoring balance — both physically and emotionally.

Pilates & Movement for All

I’m a fully accredited APPI Trained Pilates Instructor, offering 1:1 and small group sessions. Inspired by a wonderful Pilates teacher who helped me reconnect with movement and a positive mindset, I believe Pilates is for everyone — no matter your age or ability. It’s a powerful, gentle tool to support recovery, build strength, and improve confidence in your body.

My Approach: Empowering, Supportive, Patient-Centred

I work using a blend of evidence-based physiotherapy and holistic techniques, always with a warm, personalised approach. I draw on frameworks like the WASP and BRIDGES approaches, focusing on:

  • Self-efficacy and confidence building
  • Goal setting and meaningful outcomes
  • Collaborative, patient-led care

Whether you’re looking for hands-on physiotherapy, gentle holistic treatments, or movement support through Pilates, I aim to create a calm, supportive space where you can feel heard, cared for, and empowered to move forward.
